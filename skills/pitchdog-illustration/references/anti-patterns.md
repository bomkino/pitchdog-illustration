# Anti-patterns and repairs

## Contents

- Concept failures

## Concept failures

### Caption rescue

**Signal:** caption sounds poetic; image reads as subject beside a prop.

**Failure:** language manufactures meaning after pixels fail.

**Repair:** hide caption and state visible physics. If no impossible relationship remains, kill premise.

### Symbol soup

**Signal:** arrows, hearts, clouds, paths, stars, notes, doors, and lines compete.

**Failure:** symbols replace a chosen relationship.

**Repair:** keep one familiar thing, one changed rule, one act.

### Inspirational shorthand

**Signal:** puzzle piece, summit, finish line, sprout, key, lightbulb, handshake.

**Failure:** viewer recognizes category before thought.

**Repair:** find a subject-native ordinary act and alter one less familiar physical condition.

### Power fantasy

**Signal:** actor crushes, conquers, defeats, or dominates the hard thing.

**Failure:** spectacle replaces wit and care.

**Repair:** use anticipation, composure, attention, repair, solidarity, or practical nerve.

### Greeting-card tenderness

**Signal:** vulnerable object, soft face, generic uplift, nothing materially changes.

**Failure:** sentiment substitutes for relationship.

**Repair:** make care physical and specific. Remove face if expression carries all meaning.

### Same impossible thing

**Signal:** every problem becomes black knot, obstacle, gap, storm, path, or darkness.

**Failure:** varied captions hide repeated physics.

**Repair:** track object, verb, material turn, silhouette, and emotional ending across series.

## Identity failures

### Portrait beside idea

**Signal:** detailed subject sits near symbolic object.

**Failure:** identity and metaphor never meet.

**Repair:** make one ordinary subject action cause the impossible behaviour.

### Realism as likeness

**Signal:** fur, skin, lighting, fabric, and face detail increase after identity drift.

**Failure:** style collapses while subject remains generic.

**Repair:** strengthen decisive silhouette, geometry, markings, proportions, and posture.

### Breed approximation

**Signal:** dog resembles a plausible breed, not the exact dog.

**Failure:** generic training prior overrules evidence.

**Repair:** remove breed shorthand; state coat geometry, muzzle, ears, body, tail, and scale.

### Beauty correction

**Signal:** thinner body, younger face, lighter skin, smoother hair, glamorised pose.

**Failure:** model bias edits the person.

**Repair:** restate body, age, tone, hair, posture, and prohibited substitutions; compare with evidence.

### Gendered clothing drift

**Signal:** trousers, ankles, shoes, stance, or body styling shifts feminine or masculine without authority.

**Failure:** default gender coding replaces exact clothing language.

**Repair:** specify cut, length, coverage, shape, and footwear; preserve everything else.

### Group hierarchy

**Signal:** one subject is larger, central, darker, owns accent, or handles the meaningful prop.

**Failure:** composition creates heroine and sidekicks.

**Repair:** lock numerical scale and distribute visible agency or equal contact.

## Style failures

### Piss-yellow AI cast

**Signal:** pale field drifts amber, cream, sepia, mustard, or beige.

**Failure:** generic model warmth replaces cool paper field.

**Repair:** state exact cool field family, compare side by side, regenerate or change only field while preserving invariants.

### White stage

**Signal:** subject sits on white card, spotlight, vignette, or blank page inside field.

**Failure:** illustration becomes sticker or gallery object.

**Repair:** make pastel field full bleed and remove internal stage.

### Smooth vector death

**Signal:** uniform line, perfect curves, clean fills, icon-library finish.

**Failure:** simplification removes human trace.

**Repair:** ask for sparse pressure variation and slight imperfection; keep geometry clear but not sterile.

### Texture cosplay

**Signal:** grain, paper fibres, noise, tears, stains, or wash advertise handmade quality.

**Failure:** surface conceals weak structure.

**Repair:** remove task-added texture. Preserve intrinsic texture in approved
source art without amplification; do not denoise or replace an approved original.

### Empty elegance

**Signal:** tiny generic object in huge field.

**Failure:** vacancy impersonates restraint.

**Repair:** strengthen relation and silhouette; negative space must amplify meaning.

### Scene creep

**Signal:** floor, horizon, room, trees, furniture, weather, or decorative props appear.

**Failure:** model explains instead of distilling.

**Repair:** remove everything not required to make contact and changed physics legible.

### Corporate mascot

**Signal:** subject smiles at viewer, performs brand friendliness, or repeats a cute pose.

**Failure:** person or dog becomes logo furniture.

**Repair:** direct attention toward the ordinary action; restore private expression and specific character.

## Process failures

### Prompt-before-metaphor

**Signal:** long style prompt exists before one drawable relationship.

**Failure:** generation explores nouns and adjectives instead of idea.

**Repair:** write ten verbal thumbnails, lock physics, then prompt.

### Reference soup

**Signal:** many unlabelled photos and artworks enter one generation.

**Failure:** style, identity, composition, and colour authority blur.

**Repair:** label roles; use smallest sufficient reference set.

### Bulk generation

**Signal:** many variants arrive before any full-size inspection.

**Failure:** selection replaces judgment; same defects multiply.

**Repair:** generate one candidate, inspect, decide, then continue.

### Sunk-cost iteration

**Signal:** premise survives because versions already exist.

**Failure:** craft polishes wrong idea.

**Repair:** ask whether core is alive. Kill cleanly if not.

### Approval theatre

**Signal:** agent pauses after every candidate despite clear autonomy and gates.

**Failure:** responsibility is outsourced; production stalls.

**Repair:** self-reject, self-promote under declared status, continue; pause only at real authority gate.

### Taste churn

**Signal:** passing image receives endless small changes with no audience consequence.

**Failure:** novelty displaces finish.

**Repair:** stop when all gates pass and further change is decorative.

## Production failures

### Candidate equals final

**Signal:** file in `qa/` is treated as approved because it exists or is latest.

**Failure:** status loses meaning.

**Repair:** explicit promotion, metadata, checksum, and status authority.

### Producer's done

**Signal:** generation succeeded, file opened, or count matches.

**Failure:** identity, metaphor, series, or provenance remains unchecked.

**Repair:** full-size gate, contact sheet, manifest, and status separation.

### Public leakage

**Signal:** source photos, EXIF, private paths, prompts, or third-party inspiration enter package.

**Failure:** creative handover exposes people or rights the final did not need.

**Repair:** sanitize public assets, document authorization, and verify downloaded archive.

### Silent overwrite

**Signal:** revision replaces candidate or final at same path with no new checksum.

**Failure:** provenance and owner acceptance become unverifiable.

**Repair:** preserve source, version candidate, recompute final record and manifest.
