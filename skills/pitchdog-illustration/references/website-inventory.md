# Website inventory

## Purpose

Map illustration to narrative jobs before producing more. A page does not need an image because a section exists.

## Read authority first

When a website master, sitemap, wireframe, live site, or content source exists:

1. Read the complete current authority.
2. Identify exact project/version and stale sources.
3. Preserve approved copy, facts, names, pronouns, credits, and route intent.
4. Inspect current placements at phone and wide sizes where possible.
5. Inventory existing illustrations and their metaphors.

Do not invent page needs from filenames alone.

## Give each illustration a job

Useful jobs include:

- opening a proposition with one memorable relationship;
- making an abstract service truth physical;
- introducing a real person or animal without portrait theatre;
- changing emotional temperature between dense passages;
- carrying a narrative hinge;
- making labour, care, access, or shared responsibility visible;
- closing a sequence with earned tenderness;
- supporting an empty, paused, waiting, or recovery state.

Weak jobs include “add charm”, “fill space”, “make it more branded”, “break up text”, or “we need one per section”.

## Build a placement map

For each candidate need, record:

| Field | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Route and section | Where exactly will it live? |
| Audience job | What should become easier to understand, feel, or remember? |
| Nearby copy | What truth already exists and must not be repeated literally? |
| Visual opportunity | What material relationship can image reveal? |
| Existing overlap | Which approved metaphor, object, verb, or silhouette is too close? |
| Subject | Who or what should act? Is identity evidence available? |
| Canvas | Aspect ratio, rendered size, crop, and breakpoint behavior? |
| Status | Needed, optional, already served, or should remain empty? |

Rank needs by narrative consequence. Generate high-value images first, one at a time.

## Protect responsive authorship

- Choose aspect ratio from the real placement, not habit.
- Keep the meaningful lower-field motif inside safe crop at phone and wide sizes.
- Do not enlarge a tiny illustration until it becomes a hero merely to fill desktop space.
- Preserve negative space when cropping; do not crop it away as expendable margin.
- Provide descriptive alt text for meaningful images.
- Use empty alt text only when an image is truly decorative and conveys no unique meaning.
- Check load weight and choose delivery formats without changing the archived final.

## Audit the whole set

Before adding work, ask:

- Is a page missing understanding, or only decoration?
- Does another image already perform this emotional or narrative job?
- Are the same black obstacle, coral thread, bridge, doorway, knot, or light turn recurring?
- Are family members represented with equal dignity rather than quota math?
- Does the site need stillness more than another illustration?
- Does a strong existing image need a better placement instead of a replacement?

## Output

Return a compact production map:

| Priority | Route / section | Job | Subject | Metaphor territory | Canvas | Evidence | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use `Decision` values: `make`, `reuse`, `move`, `hold`, or `leave empty`.

After mapping, continue through authorized `make` items without approval pauses. Re-run the page and series audit when all images exist.
