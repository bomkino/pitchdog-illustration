# Calibration

Use these as reasoning evidence, never reusable compositions or caption syntax.

## Strong mechanisms

### Practical care changes force

`Manali gave lightning sensible shoes.`

Why it works: volatile force keeps its charge but gains one practical condition. The ordinary act is specific, visual, and unsentimental.

### Attention gives the small thing authority

`Jenai gave the smallest detail an entrance.`

Why it works: hierarchy becomes a physical doorway relation. The actor's attention, not scale spectacle, changes the system.

### A system fits life

`Kumail taught the doorway whom it was for.`

Why it works: a rigid object learns from the body moving through it. Material politics appears through fit, not a slogan.

### Patience outlasts silence

`Spotty listened until the stone ran out of silence.`

Why it works: nothing performs cuteness. Duration and attention create the impossible turn.

### Small domestic measure enters darkness

`Luna carried one teaspoon of daylight into the dark.`

Why it works: scale is precise, ordinary, and emotionally believable. Courage stays small.

### Action arrives before anxiety

`Juno carried the bridge before the gap could worry.`

Why it works: timing is the impossibility. Her action-first identity becomes anticipation, not rescue theatre.

### Shared care changes physics

`The sisters carried the heavy thing lightly.`

Why it works: one heavy shape floats because every sister carries an equal tail. Equality is visible in contact and scale.

## Useful differences

The approved set holds one visual family while varying:

- actor temperament: calm, quick, patient, nervous, action-first;
- physical rule: weight, material, timing, obedience, continuity, scale, attention;
- ordinary act: tie, seat, fold, feed, listen, carry, applaud, shelter;
- emotional temperature: wit, tenderness, composure, solidarity, practical care;
- silhouette: kneeling body, side-profile dog, shared arc, line crossing, suspended form;
- field: powder, lilac, mint, blush;
- accent role: shoes, thread, mat, bridge, doorway, sling, small object.

Family resemblance comes from judgment and visual law, not repeated nouns.

## Rejected calibration

### Rolled path

Failure: path read as carpet prop; dog remained polished portrait beside idea. More revision would polish dead premise.

Lesson: recognizable object plus likeness is not a metaphor. Actor must change physics.

### Oversized sister

Failure: one dog read roughly `1.5×`; another became visually diminished. Shared sling could not rescue hierarchy.

Lesson: numerical scale and equal contact are creative constraints, not cleanup.

### Realistic animal in pastel composition

Failure: identity improved while editorial economy and poetry collapsed.

Lesson: preserve geometry, not fur rendering.

### Smooth vector simplification

Failure: fewer details produced less life.

Lesson: economy needs imperfect human decisions, not sterile geometry.

### Bulk generation

Failure: wit, colour discipline, likeness, and selection quality fell together.

Lesson: one-image loop is part of the style.

### Forced wordplay

Failure: pun created a caption but not an inevitable picture.

Lesson: verbal cleverness must survive as material relation.

## Counterfactual checks

A strong skill can produce:

- a pitch.dog illustration with no dog;
- exact dog identity without fur realism;
- a group with distinct characters and equal dignity;
- a warm image with no smile, heart, hug, or beige;
- a brave image with no domination or spectacle;
- wit with no pun;
- a static field with no decorative motion marks;
- a current-style image that resembles no approved composition;
- an honest decision to leave a website section empty.

## Final calibration question

Does this feel like the same standard discovering a new truth, or the same template receiving new nouns?
