## Precedence

Use references as evidence, not recipes.

1. Latest explicit owner correction, current written identity lock, and current
   same-subject final.
2. Current locked 22 visual family and current written visual law.
3. Golden Six conceptual ancestry for wit and editorial economy.
4. Approved website-30 set for breadth, placement history, and collision checks.
5. General editorial-illustration knowledge.

When an early reference conflicts with current law, current law wins.

## Golden Six reading

The Golden Six established wit, a compact visual premise, imperfect ink, and the courage to let one relationship carry a frame. They predate the tighter current lock. Read each critically.

### 01 — Make the Room Lean In

- Learn: a whole group can obey one impossible physical direction; the visual joke is immediate.
- Do not inherit: yellow field, larger scene, or crowd complexity as current defaults.

### 02 — Find the Spine

- Learn: a familiar material object can embody an abstract editorial truth without labels.
- Do not inherit: dominant central scale or dense paper pile when a smaller relation can work.

### 03 — Every Slide Earns Its Place

- Learn: one object crosses from hidden group to visible agency.
- Do not inherit: white stage, spotlight, anthropomorphic-object cast, or theatrical scene.

### 04 — Make the World Visible

- Learn: one ordinary hand action can reveal an impossible contained world.
- Do not inherit: scenery, storybook detail, or multiple decorative symbols.

### 05 — Hold the Idea Steady

- Learn: a stable ordinary object can oppose a field of distraction.
- Do not inherit: arrows, hands, notes, water, or symbol density. Current work would reduce this to one relation.

### 06 — The Numbers Have People Inside

- Learn: familiar data geometry can contain a humane impossible opening; negative space gives the turn authority.
- Keep especially: tiny figure, spare field, one useful breach in an abstract system.

No Golden Six composition is a template. Their nouns, silhouettes, and captions must not be reused by substitution.

## Current locked set

The 22 current finals are the active quality gate. They establish:

- cool powder, lilac, mint, and blush fields;
- full bleed with no white stage;
- small lower-field motifs;
- controlled identity colour;
- near-black imperfect ink;
- coral or similarly restrained semantic accent;
- exact family identity through decisive cues;
- one ordinary act changing one physical rule;
- calm negative space;
- no scenery, gloss, or caption dependency.

The set includes four Manali, four Jenai, four Kumail, three Spotty, three Luna, three Juno, and one three-sisters illustration.

Use same-subject current files first for identity. Use different-subject files to prove the style can survive varied bodies, coats, objects, and emotional temperatures.

## Approved website set

The 30 website illustrations are owner-approved narrative work, not disposable
drafts. They materially broaden object, subject, page, and aspect-ratio range.
Use them to:

- avoid repeating an existing noun, verb, physical turn, silhouette, or site job;
- understand how metaphor can answer source-backed website copy;
- reuse an already approved illustration when it serves better than new art;
- learn where a concept was intended to live before proposing another.

They predate the tightest current production lock. Do not inherit a white stage,
warm cast, scene density, caption dependence, generic dog, or other visible drift
merely because an image was approved in its original context. Read [website
approved catalog](website-approved-catalog.md).

Two files have a stricter machine-readable current-use boundary in
`reference-manifest.json`:

- `28-go-get-em.png` is dated Process/metaphor evidence only. Its Juno predates
  the locked sisters scale and cannot be reused unchanged. If the Process coda
  still needs art, rebuild the sisters at Spotty `1.0`, Luna `1.0`, Juno `1.1`.
- `30-lost-page.png` keeps a strong 404 relationship, but its broad generic Juno
  is not current likeness authority. Preserve the missing-corner idea; rebuild
  Juno from current `approved-22` identity evidence before production use.

Historical owner approval remains true. It does not make stale identity pixels
current. Never silently replace these immutable dated files.

## Selection protocol

### Before concept work

1. Open the contact sheet.
2. Scan both approved catalogs for repeated objects, verbs, physical turns,
   colours, silhouettes, and site jobs to avoid.
3. Open two to four nearby finals at actual-pixel size for the active profile.

### Before generation

Choose:

- one or two current style authorities with comparable motif density;
- two to four identity references for the exact subject;
- at most one Golden Six ancestor when it teaches a relevant mechanism.

Label roles by image index. Do not feed the complete 58-image active library
into the generator. Use the smallest reference set that separates style,
identity, composition, and colour roles.

### During QA

Compare candidate against:

- one same-subject final or written identity lock;
- two current finals with different concepts;
- one Golden Six ancestor;
- contact sheet at series scale.

Actual-pixel inspection catches identity and artefacts. Contact-sheet inspection catches samey composition, colour drift, and accidental hierarchy. Escalate to the canonical profile when a portable derivative cannot settle a pixel-level question.

## Public boundary

Bundled Golden Six, website-30, and approved-22 images are released with this repository under 0BSD by owner authorization.

Excluded deliberately:

- real family photographs;
- private likeness evidence;
- rejected candidates containing unnecessary private context;
- the third-party original inspiration screenshot;
- private paths, prompts, and account data.

The bundled references permit study and reuse. They do not grant rights in the `pitch.dog` name or imply endorsement of modified work.
