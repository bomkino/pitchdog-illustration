# Code of conduct

Be exact, generous, and materially useful. Critique work without humiliating
people. Protect privacy. Credit labour. Do not use taste, status, gender, race,
class, disability, age, sexuality, nationality, body, or technical access as a
weapon.

Harassment, hate, coercion, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate
misgendering are not welcome. Neither is laundering prejudice through “brand
fit”, “professionalism”, “beauty”, or “likeness”.

Maintainers may remove contributions or participation that make the project
less safe, truthful, or useful. Report sensitive conduct concerns privately.
